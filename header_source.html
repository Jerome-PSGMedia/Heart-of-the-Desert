 50 Years of Tradition

Built by a Family. Loved by Generations.

In 1974, George and Marianne Schweers planted their first pistachio trees in the high desert of Alamogordo. What began as a family experiment has flourished into a full-grown orchard, vineyard, and tasting destination — still owned and operated by their children today.

From humble beginnings to global recognition, Heart of the Desert remains rooted in what matters most: quality, family, and the spirit of New Mexico.
